Two ways to close the gap

Same 16 photos (14 landscape, 2 portrait — outlined in orange so they're easy to spot) laid out both ways, at the same column count as the real site. Colors stand in for actual photos so the layout mechanics are easy to see.

Option 1 — Justified rows slight crop

Every row is scaled to a shared height that fills the width edge to edge — no gaps, ever. To make that math work with a portrait mixed into a row, a sliver gets trimmed off the sides of a couple photos (look closely at the edges of the outlined tiles).

Option 2 — Real masonry zero crop

Each photo drops into whichever column has the most room, at its exact natural aspect ratio — nothing is ever trimmed. Gaps only ever show up at the very bottom of a column, never in the middle of the grid.
